# Disclosure boundaries

This is a documentation-only showcase of a commercial project. The public name is generic. Text and conceptual diagrams were written for the portfolio rather than copied from a customer delivery archive.

No client names, original branding, bot handles, customer identifiers, payment addresses, credentials, deployment URLs, pricing, revenue, usage counts, source bundles, database exports, private logs, or customer screenshots are included.

The case explains selected product and engineering decisions. It does not disclose the full implementation, grant rights to private software, provide an operational runbook, or establish current production readiness. No open-source license is granted for the commercial application.

Historical engineering validation is labelled as such. Public documentation was reviewed against private records; this publication does not rerun the product or supply independently reproducible private evidence.

Please keep sensitive information out of public issues. General questions about the work can be raised through the linked portfolio inquiry.
